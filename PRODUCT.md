# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People managing their personal finances on Android who need a clear, dependable view of transactions, cash flow, goals, and AI-assisted financial insights. They often use the app in short, focused sessions and need controls, financial data, and next actions to remain immediately understandable.

## Product Purpose

FInsightAI turns personal financial activity and statements into useful, understandable intelligence. It should help users confidently review their position, identify meaningful patterns, explore insights, and take the next sensible action without adding anxiety or unnecessary complexity.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, intelligent, calm, and highly readable. The experience should feel refined and professional, with quiet confidence and purposeful feedback rather than spectacle.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered crypto-casino aesthetics, neon saturation, excessive gradients, decorative glass effects, noisy dashboards, gamified financial pressure, ambiguous controls, and motion that competes with the user's task.

## Design Principles

1. Make every action dependable: visible controls must respond clearly and lead somewhere meaningful.
2. Keep financial information calm and legible: hierarchy, contrast, and plain language should reduce cognitive load.
3. Use motion to explain state: transitions confirm navigation, loading, and completion without delaying the user.
4. Preserve Android-native expectations: gestures, hardware back behavior, touch targets, keyboard handling, and safe areas should feel natural.
5. Earn trust through consistency: navigation, feedback, loading, and interaction patterns should behave the same way throughout the app.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and readable type at supported Android display scales. Support reduced-motion preferences, do not rely on color alone to communicate state, maintain at least 44–48 dp touch targets, provide accessibility labels and selected/busy states, and keep interactions usable with Android screen readers and hardware back navigation.
